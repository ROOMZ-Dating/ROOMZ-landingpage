## HOW IT WORKS — ORGANIZERS

1. **Create a room** — Name your event, set the guest count, pick your dates.
2. **Share the link or code** — Send it out with your invites, on-site signage, or WhatsApp.
3. **Watch it happen** — Guests join, browse, and match. You get a live dashboard the whole time.

**Callout:** No app for you to manage. No tech setup. Just a link.

---

## HOW IT WORKS — GUESTS

1. **Join the room** — Scan a code or tap a link from your event organizer.
2. **Browse who's there** — See other singles at the same event as you.
3. **Send a Hey!** — If it's mutual, you match and the chat opens instantly.

**Callout:** Only people at your event can see your profile. No strangers, no swiping through a city.
